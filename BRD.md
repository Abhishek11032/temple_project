**Document Status:** Draft for Client Review

## 1. Document Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the business requirements and visitor-facing content for the Shri Khatu Shyam Ji Temple website.

It describes the temple information currently presented, the website's pages and sections, religious and cultural content, visitor information, event galleries, contact details, donation information, and potential improvements.

This BRD will serve as the primary reference for rebuilding the website from scratch.

### 1.2 Scope

The website scope includes:

- Home page
- About the Temple
- Explore Our Journey
- Gallery
- Event-specific galleries
- Temple vision and mission
- Temple construction information
- Religious events and celebrations
- Temple location and visit timings
- Contact information
- Donation information
- Navigation and footer
- Social media and WhatsApp links
- Visitor-facing navigation and gallery interactions

### 1.3 Intended Audience

- Temple trust and client stakeholders
- Business analysts
- Website designers
- Website development team
- Content reviewers

### 1.4 Source of Information

This document is based on the existing website content and the supplied analysis of its pages, sections, gallery records, contact information, donation details, and links.

### 1.5 Limitations

The historical statements, religious claims, event details, contact information, timings, donation information, and map location have not been independently verified.

Information that conflicts across website sections must be confirmed by the temple trust before being treated as final.

## 2. Temple Overview

### 2.1 Temple Name

The primary temple name used on the website is:

**Shri Khatu Shyam Ji Temple**

Other names appearing in the existing content include:

- Shri Khatu Shyam Temple
- Khatu Shyam Temple Project
- Shiv Shyam Khatu Mandir Dev Bhumi Trust

The trust must confirm the official temple and trust names for consistent use across the website.

### 2.2 Introduction and Project Background

The website presents Shri Khatu Shyam Ji Temple as a devotional initiative focused on faith, devotion, Sanatan culture, and community service.

The Home page invites devotees to participate in the temple's construction and service journey.

The About section describes a proposed Khatu Shyam Ji Temple within the premises of an ancient Shiva temple. Other sections describe the temple as under construction.

### 2.3 Location

The existing website gives the temple location as:

Sarasara, Varanasi Mandal, Uttar Pradesh, near Barsathi railway station, approximately 50 km from Varanasi.

The embedded map is labelled **Temple Of Shiva** and the directions link uses these coordinates:

- Latitude: 25.5651967
- Longitude: 82.4679202

The exact relationship between the Khatu Shyam Ji Temple project and the Shiva temple is described differently across pages and requires confirmation.

### 2.4 Historical Background

The About page states that the associated Shiva temple is approximately one thousand years old and refers to a self-manifested Shiva lingam discovered during excavation of a pond.

The Explore Our Journey page describes an ancient Shiva temple believed by devotees to have existed for nearly one thousand years.

These are statements presented by the website and have not been independently verified.

### 2.5 Religious Significance

The website describes Khatu Shyam Ji in terms of:

- Compassion
- Courage
- Unconditional devotion
- Peace and prosperity
- Spiritual strength
- Faith and blessings
- Hope and happiness

The website also states that devotees have experienced miracles and blessings. These statements represent the religious messaging used by the website and should be presented in the appropriate devotional context.

### 2.6 Vision

The website's stated vision is to establish Shri Khatu Shyam Temple as a sacred destination where spirituality, culture, and devotion unite.

The vision also emphasizes preserving Sanatan traditions and inspiring future generations to remain connected with faith, values, and service.

### 2.7 Mission and Objectives

The existing website lists these activities:

1. Daily Worship & Aarti
2. Religious Events & Celebrations
3. Charity Activities
4. Community Service
5. Spiritual Guidance
6. Cultural Programs

These activities are stated objectives. The website does not provide complete schedules or operational details confirming that each activity is currently available.

## 14. Executive Summary

The Shri Khatu Shyam Ji Temple website currently serves as a devotional and informational platform. It introduces the temple project, presents the stated vision and mission, describes religious background, showcases event photographs, provides location and contact information, and communicates donation purposes and contribution details.

The primary business requirements are to:

- Communicate the temple's identity and spiritual purpose.
- Present the available temple background and mission.
- Provide event information and photo galleries.
- Help visitors find location and timings.
- Enable visitors to contact the trust.
- Present donation purposes and available contribution information.
- Provide clear access to the website's information and visitor-facing actions.

This BRD is the starting point for rebuilding the website from scratch. The new implementation should follow the approved business requirements and reuse the existing image assets without reusing the old website's UI implementation.
